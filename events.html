14 May 2025 · Tokyo, Japan

Lithuania–Japan Business Seminar

Participation in the Lithuanian business mission and JETRO seminar, focused on commercial ties, smart-city technology and opportunities for cooperation between Lithuania and Japan.

Senior programme participants: Lukas Savickas — Lithuania’s Minister of Economy and Innovation; Romualda Stragienė — CEO, Innovation Agency Lithuania; Jeremy Cowx — Managing Director, Teltonika Japan; Tomas Ridikas — Sales Director, Solet. JETRO and the Lithuanian Embassy in Japan co-organised the seminar.

15 May 2025 · Osaka, Japan

EXPO 2025 — Smart City Solutions

Presentation on smart-city solutions and international technology partnerships at the Lithuania-led EXPO 2025 programme, Smart City Solutions: Bridging Collaborations Between Lithuania and Partners Worldwide.

Official programme: Minister Lukas Savickas; moderator Andrius Karazinas; speakers Anu Eslas, Julius Sakalauskas, Austė Pranckutė and Indrė Ubartaitė. The programme included city and industry representatives from Lithuania and Japan.
